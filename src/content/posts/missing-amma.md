Sometimes all you can do is wonder what you are where you are
how you are there aren't any answers to these because these are
not the questions that you are looking answers for. You are seeking
meaning meaning out of life meaning out of your mundane routine but
it's okay to give in to the grind, it's way easier to be someone that
follows and way difficult to be someone that leads. Following although
some people will say isn't something you should do but remember it's
okay just find a person worth following, not everyone is meant to be a
leader.

I have spent my whole life wondering and wandering here and there, maybe
if I had done something differently i would be somewhere better but the
thing is I am right where I want to be, my friends and family think I am
joking when I say this but trust me I am not, I spent nearly entirety of
my life viewing happiness through my mother's eyes the spark in her eyes
made me feel that maybe I was doing something right, that maybe if she
stayed happy I would be happy, I used to think i will be happy as long as
I could see her smile, but then one morning she wasn't there the doctor
said you were late to bring her to the hospital.

For a few years, idk three or four years I absolutely had no idea what I was
doing I would go to work, I would sit and do something that I can't recall
anymore, work became an escape and that's all I needed a escape i enrolled
myself into a post grad programme which I am not sure how I did, it's all
hazy, I feel it helped me or I would like to think it helped me, but you can
only treat life as a distraction for so long. It felt like I was awake but
I was asleep to everything around me.

By the time I woke up I felt it was too late, the world had moved on I had
done irreperable damage to my life to my career to everything i once held dear,
don't get me wrong all that was close to me lost it's charm on that day when they
pronounced her dead, death is something that's absolute and that's what I like about
life the absolute end that this will end that this is not permanent. I will
maybe one day look back and wonder what I did wrong why i did wrong and place
the blame on my mothers death but that's something i don't want to do i
have to take full responsibility for my actions and I have to improve myself.

As that Oasis song goes "times are hard when things have got no meaning" but I
finally feel i have found the meaning mostly in mundane in seeing my family happy
in learning new things, in learning how to learn, joys that I find now are not
for the things I wanted when my amma was alive. The things I wanted then moved
on without me now is the time for me to move on as well moving on doesn't mean
that I will stop missing her, she will always be with me but I feel she will be
happy if I did something different in life.

I will forever miss amma but maybe I can start living a little now.
